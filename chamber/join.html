NP Membership Benefits

No membership fee for nonprofit organizations.

  • Access to chamber community information
  • Opportunities to connect with local organizations
  • Invitations to selected community events

Bronze Membership Benefits

Our introductory paid membership level.

  • Chamber networking event invitations
  • Business directory listing
  • Access to basic member resources

Silver Membership Benefits

A higher-tier membership with additional opportunities.

  • All Bronze Membership benefits
  • Discounts on selected chamber events
  • Professional development and training opportunities
  • Eligibility for featured business spotlights

Gold Membership Benefits

Our highest paid membership level.

  • All Silver Membership benefits
  • Priority consideration for homepage spotlights
  • Expanded business promotion opportunities
  • Priority registration for selected chamber events
